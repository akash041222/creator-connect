FAQ

Common questions

How do payments actually work? +

Brands mark a payment as approved after reviewing submitted content, then release it as paid. CreatorConnect tracks the full history and generates an invoice — no external gateway required to get started.

Can I apply to a campaign I don't fully qualify for? +

You can apply to any open campaign, but brands see your follower count and category against their stated minimums, so honest profiles perform better.

What happens if a brand rejects my submission? +

A brand can approve, reject, or request changes. Requested changes let you resubmit without losing the accepted application.

How does verification work? +

Admins review company and creator profiles and grant a verified badge once identity and activity checks pass.
